How it works
1
Before a ball is struck
One quarter-finalist from each of the eight sections, then narrow to four, two and a champion. Nominate a top-ten seed to flop and an unseeded player to run.
2
Round by round
A new set of bets opens with each round. Call the featured match, its set score, and whatever else the organiser has cooked up.
3
The deadline locks it
When a round closes, everyone's picks become visible at once. No late edits, no excuses, no screenshots of what you meant to send.
4
Points, then bragging rights
The organiser enters each result once. Every score recalculates with a line explaining exactly where your points came from.
